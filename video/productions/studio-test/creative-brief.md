# Creative brief: Studio test video

- Audience: the app owner and anyone checking that the studio works.
- One-sentence message: the studio can take a video from research to an audited file.
- Tone: plain and factual.
- Look: sunset palette, Bahnschrift titles, cut-edge cards, moving night backdrop.
- Must say: unofficial fan project; the voice is test narration.
- Must not say: anything about the game.
