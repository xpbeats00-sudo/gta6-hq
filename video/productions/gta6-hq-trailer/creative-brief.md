# Creative brief: GTA 6 HQ trailer

- Audience: people waiting for Grand Theft Auto VI who would use a desktop encyclopedia and manual.
- One-sentence message: GTA 6 HQ is a fan-made encyclopedia and Field Manual in one app, and here is what is in it.
- Kind and format: game trailer, 16:9, 1920x1080, 30 frames a second, 60 seconds.
- Tone: confident and quick. The app speaks for itself; the titles only name what is on screen.
- Look: the app's own look. Sunset palette `#f7a13c` / `#ee3f7c` / `#7c3fd6`, Bahnschrift display type, cut-edge cards, the slanted wipe that echoes the app's cast cards.
- Footage: the real app and its real installer, filmed over the DevTools port. No mock-ups.
- Music: the beat the owner supplied, 135 BPM. Every cut sits on a bar line.

## Structure

| Bars | Time | Music | Picture |
|---|---|---|---|
| 0 to 6 | 0 to 11 s | Quiet opening | The app's splash, then the title |
| 6 to 14 | 11 to 25 s | The beat comes in | Home, Encyclopedia, Characters, Map: one every two bars |
| 14 to 23 | 25 to 41 s | Drops back | Field Manual, the numbers, Vehicles |
| 23 to 29 | 41 to 51 s | The beat returns | The installer, then where to get it |
| 29 to end | 51 to 60 s | Fades out | Credits and the notice |

## Must say

- "Unofficial fan project", held in the corner of every shot and stated in the title and the credits.
- Where to get it.

## Must not say

- Anything about the game that is not on the app's own pages.
- "Official", "release date", "leak", "free", "open source".
- No Rockstar Games logo. No imagery from earlier games.
