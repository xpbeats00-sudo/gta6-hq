GTA 6 HQ is an unofficial fan project.

It is not published, approved or endorsed by Rockstar Games or Take-Two Interactive. Grand Theft Auto and related names, screenshots, artwork and footage belong to their owners.

What the app does on your computer:

- It installs for your user account only. No administrator access is needed.
- It needs the Microsoft Edge WebView2 Runtime, which Windows 11 already has.
- When it opens, it asks GitHub whether a newer version exists and shows a notice if so. Nothing is downloaded or installed automatically.
- The map downloads its tiles from gta6-wiki.org the first time each area is viewed, and keeps them in a cache folder beside the app.
- If Discord is running, it can show "GTA 6 HQ" and the page you are reading on your Discord profile.

The update check and the Discord status can each be turned off in the app's data folder (update.json and discord.json). There is no telemetry and no account.
