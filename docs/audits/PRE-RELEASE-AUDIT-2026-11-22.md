# Pre-release material audit

**Date:** 2026-11-22. **Question:** does anything in GTA 6 HQ still rest only on material from before the game came out (19 November 2026)?

**Short answer:** Not in the encyclopedia's facts or the Field Manual's text. What is left on pre-release sources only is:
- **Dated news history:** 20 items.
- **Fan-identified lists:** 4 lists from Fandom.
- **Three businesses:** no guide has covered them since release.
- **Pictures:** Rockstar's pre-release pictures, kept by the owner's rule.

## How it was checked

- **Encyclopedia:** each of the 3,150 entries was checked against the date its sources were read.
  - A source counts as pre-release if it was read before 16 November or comes from the preview file.
  - That covers gta6-wiki.org, the Fandom category pages, TGG's pre-launch guide and Rockstar's pre-launch Newswire posts.
- **Wording:**
  - Every entry was searched for pre-release phrasing, such as "will be", "pre-orders include", "has not been announced", "before launch" and "trailer".
  - The UI code was also searched for countdown text.
- **Field Manual:** every block's evidence label was counted, looking for preview-reported, developer-described and footage-only blocks.
- **Pictures:** media records whose source is a trailer, the Extended Look or Rockstar's pre-launch screenshots were counted.
- **Research:**
  - Four research passes checked the flagged items against guides and reviews of the released game.
  - Sources used: PowerPyx, GTABase, OutputLag, PC Gamer, GamesRadar+, VGC, GTA BOOM, Kotaku, KeenGamer, Cultured Vultures and VICE.
  - No banned site was newly cited.
  - Samples were re-read by hand: the Taci Ragno, the Whiffler Airport Bus, the Sirius Transgressor, the five Criminal Profile tiers and the six-star wanted meter. All matched.

## What was found and changed

| Area | Found | Changed |
|---|---|---|
| Vehicles named from trailers | 63 entries said "GTABase built its list before launch … not checked against the released game" | 22 confirmed by GTABase since release; 15 renamed to their released names (old name kept as an alias so search still finds it); 26 now say no guide since release lists them under that name |
| Gameplay and activity entries from previews | 25 entries rested on TGG's preview account or the pre-release wiki | All 25 rewritten from post-launch guides, with new sources |
| Core entries on the pre-release wiki | 44 characters, places, vehicle types, animals, businesses, factions, music, editions and FAQ answers | 41 now carry facts from guides written since release; 1 correction (see below); 3 had nothing new to add |
| Field Manual preview and interview blocks | 40 blocks with no launch-era text | 33 rewritten from launch sources; 7 moved into each page's "Unknown / to verify" box |
| Field Manual footage notes | 49 blocks describing Rockstar's trailers and screenshots | Kept as descriptions of official material; one now-misleading note ("no frame shows six stars lit") replaced with the released game's six-star facts |
| Stale wording | FAQ and edition text written in the future tense | Put into past or present tense: PC version, GTA Online, Xbox One streaming, delays, pre-order bonuses, the GTA+ month; the credits caveat that no longer applied was removed |

Everything added from guides of the released game is marked as an inline spoiler (`||…||`) on entries that are not already spoiler entries.

### Where the released game differs from the previews

- **Criminal Profile:**
  - It has five tiers: Professional, Hardened, Violent, Psycho and Irredeemable. Previews said four, with "Aggressive" as the second.
  - Kills move it, not restraint (OutputLag, PC Gamer).
- **Fences:**
  - Goods fences buy narcotics, jewelry and marked bills; only cars go to separate buyers. Previews said one fence each for drugs, jewelry and cars (GamesRadar+, GTABase).
  - A fence shows on the map as a green icon with their initials, not a dollar sign (KeenGamer).
- **Trackers:** removed at the Pay 'n' Spray once the story unlocks it. Previews said you needed a fence you had worked for (PC Gamer).
- **Controls:** full layouts have now been published (GTABase, OutputLag).
- **App names:** spelled BuckMe, WhatUp and WAiNK (GTABase).
- **Relationship:** it can stay platonic, but GTABase says the good endings need the top level, Hot Together.
- **Ultimate Edition:** six shops, not four (VGC).
- **Vehicle names:**
  - The Faggio is the Taci Ragno.
  - The Benefactor Schafter is the Benefactor Schafter V12.
  - The Brute Stockade is the Interstate Stockade '99.
  - The Mammoth Streamer216 is the Lapwing Streamer 216-C.
  - 11 others were also renamed.

## What could not be confirmed

- **Vehicles (25):** no guide since release lists these under these names. They are flagged in each entry.
  - A 26th, listed as the "Buster", was a name traced only to the 2022 leak footage, which the app never uses. Its entry, address and picture file are now the Whiffler Bus, GTABase's released name for the city bus. The picture, an official Extended Look frame, is kept.
  - Pegassi Bati 801, Grotti Bestia GTS, Bison 1800 (Police), Zoot, Willard Faction Custom Donk, JoBuilt Flatbed, Montagne Pickup (Lifeguard), Police Montagne, Amloco, Granger Police, Granger Taxi.
  - Grotti Itali GTO, Buckingham Luxor, Metromule, Vysser Neo, L35P Police Boat, Police Buzzard, Police Ghost (Stanier LE), Police Landstalker XL, Sovereign 107 Police, Canis Terminus, Brute Tipper (6 wheels), Tow Truck, Utility Truck, Buckingham Windsor.
  - gta.wiki says ZOOT is a scooter-rental brand, not a vehicle.
- **Field Manual (7 claims):** moved to "Unknown / to verify":
  - driving weight compared with GTA IV and V;
  - top speeds and supercars off-road;
  - leaving a robbery by a back door;
  - robberies inside missions working like open-world ones;
  - the yellow X;
  - hit-marker colours;
  - recoil and pistol fire rate.
- **Businesses (3):** Allied Crystal, the Tisha-Wocka flea market and Fleeca still rest on the pre-release wiki alone. No guide read since release covers them.

## What stays, and why

- **Pictures from Rockstar's pre-release trailers and screenshots (about 390):** they are official and accurate. The owner's rule is that pictures are never removed.
- **News history (20 items):** trailers 1 and 2, both delays, pre-orders, pre-load, the Extended Look, the score, the album and similar. These are dated records of what happened before launch, not claims about the game. They could be hidden if the owner prefers.
- **Fan-identified lists (4):** vehicles, animals, brands and weapons from the GTA Wiki on Fandom, read on 13 November. Fandom is on the do-not-use list, so these lists can only be replaced or removed, not re-sourced. That is the owner's call.
- **Footage notes in the Law Enforcement chapter:** the timed descriptions of the Extended Look stay as records of Rockstar's own footage. Each page now also gives the released game's meaning from launch guides.
- **Mixed sources:** 62 entries cite both pre-release and post-launch sources. The pre-release source supports the official description, and the launch source supports the released-game facts.
