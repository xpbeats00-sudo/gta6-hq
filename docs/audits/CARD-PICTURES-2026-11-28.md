# Card pictures audit, 2026-11-28

This audit grades every card face and every set cover for size, sharpness, upscaling and watermarks. A card face is the `facts.menu_picture` of one of the 480 `Collectible card (...)` entries in `data/wiki/collectibles*.json`. A set cover is one of the `images/collectibles/card-set-*.jpg` files.

**Update, same day:** the owner chose to restore the 468 MetaForge faces graded F below until clean replacements exist. Their files, `facts.menu_picture` fields and `data/media.json` records are back as they were. They still carry the faint MetaForge watermark. The clean way to replace them is the owner's own full-size captures of each set's screen in the game. The "Removed" actions recorded below were undone.

The owner's rule: no watermarked pictures in the app. A watermark is never removed, cropped out, painted over or blurred. Instead the picture is replaced with a clean copy. If no clean copy exists, the face is dropped and the card falls back to its drawn face.

## Method

- **Size:** Pillow `Image.size`.
- **Sharpness:** the variance of the 4-neighbour Laplacian on the greyscale picture, computed with numpy. A higher score is sharper. Flat graphic covers score low by nature.
- **Upscale ratio ("2x keep"):** the picture is scaled down 2x and back up with Lanczos, and the Laplacian variance afterwards is divided by the original's. About 0.2 means native detail. About 0.9 means the picture had no detail finer than half its size, so it was upscaled about 2x or more.
- **Watermark:** the source recorded in data/media.json, plus a visual check. I checked all 468 MetaForge faces on contact sheets of high-pass-filtered centre crops. I checked the 12 crops and 18 covers both as whole pictures and as high-pass versions.
