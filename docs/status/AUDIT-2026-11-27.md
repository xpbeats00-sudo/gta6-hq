## Fixed

- **Two vehicles were listed twice.** The pre-launch entries *Pegassi Faggio* and *Western Zombie Chopper* had been renamed to their release names, so they sat beside the release entries *Taci Ragno* and *Western Zombie Bobber*. Each pair is now one entry: the release entry keeps its picture and gains the old entry's stats, price, map spawn, sources and old name as an alias.
- **Old addresses keep working.** A merged entry now records its old slugs in a new `former_slugs` field, and opening an old address (from a saved bookmark or an old link) goes straight to the entry it became. This also covers the two entries merged in the picture clean-up (*benefactor-schutzen* → *Benefactor Shutzen*, *brute-b3000-excavator* → *HVY Excavator*). The validator knows the field and reports an old slug that is also a live slug (`former-slug-taken`), with a test.
- **Map wording was out of date.** The map's side panel and credit line still said most pins come from Gamersky's map and are approximate. Since 0.2.48, 5,548 of the 6,585 pins come from GTABase and sit on the street map exactly; the wording now says so. The Discord assistant now names the right source for each pin and stops calling GTABase pins approximate.
- **A spoiler leaked in the Discord assistant.** For a spoiler vehicle such as *Martha's Vamos*, the "On the map" line named the pin ("Declasse Martha's Vamos") outside the spoiler bars. Those names now go behind the bars when the entry is a spoiler.
- **Missing alt text** for the palm-tree menu background (`images/ui/menu-palms.webp`).
- **The media audit over-counted unused files.** It said 110 files were unused because it did not read every interface file, the map's blip names or the wallpaper posters. It now does, and finds 26 truly unused files (listed below).

## Second pass: icons, pictures and More (27 November 2026, later)

### What changed

- **Map icons.** Every kind of place on the map now has an icon (before: 39 kinds showed a plain coloured badge). 15 more of the game's own blips come from GTABase's map icon set; the kinds GTABase marks with a symbol (police and fire stations, hospitals, towns, Easter eggs, random events and others) get a pin of the same shape drawn from that symbol. The symbols are Material Symbols (Apache 2.0) and game-icons.net (CC BY 3.0), so these 27 pins are recorded as cleared.
