## Changed by the fact-check

- Added IGN's interview as the primary source; 24 blocks now rest on it.
- Removed: the fan wiki as a source for this chapter; the police helicopter entry; a weapon caution found in no source; "for the first time in the series".
- Downgraded: weapon and camera clues, mask, respray and "removes a star" to interpretation; the two TGG-relayed Nelson quotes to preview-reported; the whole-map search and patrol car equipment to explicit hearsay.
- Reworded three entries that were too close to one guide's phrasing.
- "Hot area" is now credited to Nelson, with VICE's "active search area" noted beside it.

## Deliberately left out

- Any numbers: thresholds, timers, radii, ranges, reaction times. The one exception is Nelson's own "three stars in the city" example.
- A unit-by-star response ladder.
- The game's Heat icon artwork. Numbered entries stand in for it.
- Anything carried over from Grand Theft Auto V or earlier.

## Open conflicts

- **What clears heat.** IGN has a new outfit removing heat; a Famitsu interview, read only through a translation summary, suggests heat may not clear while you remain in the hot area. Listed as unknown.
- **Splitting up.** Nelson: it takes some heat off. Creators and guides: it removes a star. The manual uses Nelson's wording and shows the other as interpretation.
