# Fact-check: wanted stars and Heat icons (Law Enforcement chapter)

Checked 2026-11-12, before release, by an independent reviewer. Nothing in the manual or the app was edited.

Checked against:

- the contact sheets in `docs/manual/research/frames/` (named below as scan, A, B, C, D and context) and the crops in `images/manual/footage/`;
- IGN's interview with Rob Nelson (Yahoo copy), GamesRadar+ (Iain Wilson), GTABase and VICE, each opened on 2026-11-12.

Times are seconds from the start of the file, with mm:ss in brackets.

## Summary

33 items checked across five pages.

No claim about the stars or icons is wrong in substance. Every star count, colour, icon shape and icon count the manual gives matches the frames, and every crop's file-name time matches the sheet frame it was cut from. The problems are:

1. **One time range is not supported.** Gray stars are given as "about 11:48 to 12:08". The sheets show them at 708 to 712 s and again at 728 s, and show no wanted display in six frames between and around those (714, 716, 717, 720, 722, 724, 726, 730).
2. **Scene descriptions go beyond the frames** in four places: "firefight", "ride away", "turn red as the car goes into a parking garage", "leave it on foot".
3. **Three guide attributions are slightly wider than the guides.** GamesRadar+ does not say gray means "not in sight", and does not mention a search area for red.
4. **Two "demonstrated" blocks carry a guide's reading with no guide in their sources.**
5. **Five frames print with no credit line of their own**, and the pair credit has no time.

## Are gray and hollow stars different?

Yes. They are two different drawings, not the same star over different backgrounds.

Both crops sit on a pale background, so the comparison is fair (`stars-gray-1148.jpg`, off-white ceiling; `stars-hollow-1808.jpg`, near-white sky).

- **Gray (708 s):** the two lit stars are filled mid-gray with a thin dark outline. There is no white ring. They are close in tone to the four placeholders, only slightly darker and outlined.
- **Hollow (1088 s):** the two lit stars have a dark navy centre, a white ring inside a dark outer edge. They are much darker in the middle than the placeholders beside them.

The difference holds over other backgrounds too: gray stars stay gray-filled over a mid-toned wall (sheet A 711), and hollow stars keep the white ring and dark centre over palms and over sky (sheet D 1084, 1085 to 1091).

Two further points:

- "Hollow" is slightly loose. The centre is not see-through in any frame; it is dark-filled. GamesRadar+ calls the same look "Black and White Stars". "Outlined" in the manual's alt text is accurate.
- The two also differ in what sits beneath them: five icons under gray, none under hollow. That is two sightings only, not a rule.

## Other observations

- The opening page of the chapter still warns that the chapter's photographs "are not evidence for the rule printed beside them". That is now untrue of the footage frames. Suggest limiting it to "official screenshots".
- `footage-store-1148.jpg`, `footage-garage-1559.jpg`, `footage-jewelry-1806.jpg` and `stars-two-white-1840.jpg` are on disk but not referenced in `data/manual.json`.
- Not in the manual, but visible: two white stars with only three icons (hanger, head, car; no two-heads icon) at 1104 to 1108 s (18:24 to 18:28), then four icons at 1120 to 1124 s (18:40 to 18:44).
- No caption or paragraph presents a guide author's reading as Rockstar's statement. Each guide reading is labelled "interpretation" and says so in the text.

## What I could not check

- `docs/manual/research/STARS-EVIDENCE.md` did not exist when I finished, so there is no comparison with it.
- I did not watch the video. Everything is from still frames 1 to 4 seconds apart, so I cannot confirm or rule out flashing, the moment any star changes colour, or anything shorter than the gap between frames.
- There are whole-frame context images for only twelve moments. For the gray stars at 12:08, the white stars other than 14:08 and 15:36, and the red stars after 16:02, I saw only the top-right corner, so I could not check the scene.
- The 15:58 context frame (a weapon wheel is open) is too small to count the icons reliably.
- The GamesRadar+ and Yahoo pages did not load in the fetch tool and were read from the saved page HTML instead. The text is the same articles; I could not see GamesRadar+'s annotated icon image, so I did not check its names for the six icons.
- Whether the outlined stars in the video are what Nelson calls "hollow white stars" cannot be settled from these sources; it is a match by appearance.
