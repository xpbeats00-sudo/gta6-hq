# Unknown Mechanics: police, wanted level, Heat

Agent A, 2026-11-11. This is what the evidence reviewed in `PRIMARY-SOURCE-REGISTER.md` does **not** establish. None of it should appear in the manual as fact before it is tested in the released game. Claim IDs refer to `MECHANICS-EVIDENCE-MATRIX.md`.

## Limits of this research pass

- The official Extended Look was not viewed (sign-in gate; and video cannot be watched in this environment). Nothing in the manual can honestly be labelled "demonstrated" on Agent A's authority.
- Rockstar's own site and Newswire returned no readable text about police mechanics.
- Edge's cover story (print) and IGN's written demo preview were not read.
- Two widely repeated Rob Nelson quotes exist only as read out by one creator.
- Everything Nelson said describes a July 2026 build.

## Wanted level

- What it takes to reach any star. No crime-to-star table exists.
- What each star changes: unit types, numbers, aggression, tactics.
- Whether a sixth star is reachable in ordinary free roam, and what it brings.
- Whether the solid, flashing and red star states mean what one creator says they mean. Only the hollow state is confirmed by the developer (A-09).
- Whether a hollow-star response can turn into an identified one, and what triggers that.
- How and how fast stars decay.
- Whether stars are tied to a region the way Nelson's map demonstration suggests, at every level or only at low ones.

## Crime reporting and detection

- Whether an officer seeing a crime counts as the "witness".
- Whether a witness's call can be stopped, and how.
- How far witnesses can see or hear; whether gunfire alone is reported.
- Which buildings and vehicles have alarms.
- The speed below which an advanced tracker gives your position away.
- Whether stealing a tracked vehicle unseen produces a report by itself.
- Whether police vehicles really carry trackers (one unattributed outlet).
- Where security cameras are, what they record, how long a recording matters, and whether cameras can be disabled beforehand.
- What "dispatch is specific" covers in practice.

## Heat and identification

- The complete set of Heat icons. Five are described by IGN; a weapon icon rests on one creator and one guide; the developer names only vehicle and couple from the icons, plus appearance and clothing in speech.
- What the weapon icon tracks, if it exists: weapon type, a specific weapon, or simply being armed.
- What "appearance" includes: face, hair, build, tattoos.
- What identifies a vehicle: model, colour, plate, damage.
- Whether companions other than Jason or Lucia can trigger the companion icon.
- Whether more icons make escape harder in a cumulative way.
- How long each piece of information is remembered. No timer is published.
- Whether information persists after the stars are gone (one creator says so).
- Whether "heat" in Nelson's usage is one system or two (information held, and an area being searched). See conflict 6 in the matrix.
- Whether the Criminal Profile affects police behaviour at all.

## Search areas

- Size, shape and duration of a hot area at any wanted level.
- Whether the inner and outer zones one creator describes behave differently.
- What happens on re-entry: how close, how soon, and whether stars return.
- Whether the search really becomes map-wide at six stars ("I was told", no named speaker).
- How long police remain round a robbed store.
- Why, in the demo, police began searching an area after Nelson had "got away".

## Focus and the minimap

- Range, duration, cost, cooldown and availability of Focus.
- Whether Focus shows police through walls (one creator's statement).
- Whether police are absent from the radar at every wanted level and outside chases. The relayed quote itself says "depending on the wanted level".
- Whether any police information ever appears on the minimap.
- Whether the minimap is obscured at high levels (one guide's guess).
- How far officers see and hear, and how quickly they react. Any "vision cone" numbers.

## Evasion

- Whether a new outfit clears everything or only the clothing information, and whether it works while in sight or at high wanted levels.
- Whether partial changes (jacket only, hat only) ever count toward clothing.
- Whether a haircut clears the appearance icon. Nelson says only that it does not address clothing.
- Which face coverings count, and whether a mask prevents an appearance description. Rockstar has not stated the effect.
- Whether the personal vehicle can be called while wanted, how long it takes, and how many outfits it holds.
- Exactly when the vehicle clue clears: on stepping out, on destroying the car, or on entering another.
- Whether a respray clears the vehicle clue.
- Whether splitting up changes the star count or only the Heat information.
- How the partner behaves when sent away, and how likely the partner is to be caught.
- How long "waiting for it to die down" takes, and where it is safe to wait.
- Whether interiors, alleys, water or rooftops are treated differently by the search.
- Whether stashing or dropping a weapon has any effect.
- Whether anything clears Heat instantly. No source reviewed supports the word.

## Police response

- A response ladder of any kind.
- Helicopters: no acceptable source reviewed establishes their behaviour. The drafted helicopter claim rests on a fan wiki's description of a screenshot.
- Roadblocks, spike strips, tactical teams, boats, dogs, federal or military units.
- Police vehicle roster and the departments that exist.
- Whether police cars carry weapons or armour in the trunk (one creator, "I believe").
- Arrest behaviour: surrender, warnings, non-lethal force, how an arrest is triggered.
- What the reported police warning for carrying a long gun leads to.

## Consequences

- What "Busted" costs beyond a restart. One interview, read in translation, says cash carried is at risk; nothing covers weapons, ammunition, vehicle, stolen goods or mission progress.
- Where you respawn.
- What happens to the partner when one character is arrested, beyond the fact that it counts as Busted.
- Whether being Wasted differs from being Busted.
- Any lasting record: bounties, fines, later recognition.

## Missions and online

- Which missions suspend or script the wanted system. Nelson says only that missions try to stay consistent with the open world.
- Whether character switching is available while wanted. Nelson says switching is sometimes unavailable for story reasons.
- Any online or multiplayer behaviour. Nothing has been published.
