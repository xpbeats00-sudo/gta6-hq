# Gameplay Evidence: health, food, robberies, combat, theft, switching, phone, money, progression

Compiled 2026-11-12 (seven days before release). Everything here is pre-release and describes preview builds (a July 2026 build for most sources; Edge saw a later build "a few weeks before" release). Nothing is carried over from earlier Rockstar games. Where a source itself makes a comparison ("like RDR2"), it is reported as that source's comparison.

No video footage or images were viewed. Video sources were read as caption text only.

## Labels

- `developer-described`: a named Rockstar developer said it. "(relayed)" means the words reach us through a visitor reading his own notes or transcript, not a published transcript. "(tr.)" means I read it in Japanese and the English is my own translation.
- `preview-reported`: someone who personally watched a preview build reports it.
- `interpretation`: a writer's reading of footage or of other people's reports.
- `unknown`: raised but not established.

"Nelson" is Rob Nelson, Head of Development and Co-Studio Head, Rockstar North. All sessions were **hands-off**: Nelson played, visitors watched.

## (b) Per-topic summary: what is safe to print

1. **Health.** Safe: eating gives a substantial health buff (`developer-described`, Nelson, S1). Food buffs push a gauge above its normal maximum, and the extra is lost once life falls below normal (`developer-described`, Nelson in Famitsu, translated). Health, stamina and Focus are shown as three bars at top left (`preview-reported`, one creator). Base health can be raised by training (`developer-described`). Not safe: any statement that health regenerates by itself. The only regeneration statement is Nelson, relayed, saying rest affects "regens of certain attributes". Adrenaline is named by Nelson but unexplained.
2. **Armour.** Nothing safe beyond: one creator believes police trunks hold long guns and that looting them gives body armour (`preview-reported`, hedged). No developer has described player armour.
3. **Stamina.** Safe: a stamina bar exists (`preview-reported`); energy drinks or coffee buff it and training raises its base (`developer-described`); it drains during gym sets (`developer-described`); in water it becomes a lung-capacity meter (`preview-reported`, one creator). Not established: sprint drain, sprint button (conflicting), exhaustion.
4. **Food.** Safe: fridge at the safehouse; snacks and drinks bought in shops; a drive-thru; gym vending machine; overeating adds weight; the grocery-restocking idea was cut (`developer-described` and `preview-reported` as marked). No carrying limits, prices or food list.
5. **Robberies.** Well supported by Nelson: almost any shop can be robbed, alone or together; robbery targets on a map; safes opened by found combination or by a loot-damaging explosive; police gather out front; back doors sometimes; quiet entry with a snap gun, cameras and alarms can be dealt with; loot sold to fences; preparation (a getaway vehicle) matters. Duffel bags and vehicle capacity are `preview-reported`. Heists as a structured mode, planning and crews: `unknown`.
6. **Combat.** Safe: non-lethal options and the yellow X (`developer-described`); long guns visible and carried in limited numbers, two concealed handguns (`developer-described`, partly relayed); weapons kept in the personal vehicle trunk and Ammu-Nation lockers (`developer-described`); suppressing fire (`developer-described`, translated); looser aim assist, cover on R1, crouch (`developer-described`, relayed); hit-marker colours and slow-motion kill camera (`preview-reported`). Weapon wheel: no usable evidence.
7. **Stealth.** Safe: masks are worn for robberies and removing one afterwards lowers recognition (`developer-described`); crouching out of sight was seen (`preview-reported`); quiet break-ins are possible (`developer-described`). No evidence for silenced weapons or a detection indicator.
8. **Stealing cars.** Strongly supported by Nelson: tools (slim jim, key cloner), progression, scanner, alarms, trackers, fences, Pay n' Spray, registration, protected areas, impound. App name WAINK is `preview-reported` by three attendees.
9. **Switching.** Safe: open as much as possible, restricted by the story at times; the other character follows a routine, can be texted or called, will come to you, drives if you switch seats (`developer-described`). Timing of the switch itself: not established.
10. **Phone.** Safe: physical object; Snapmatic browse-only; send photos to partner only; video, fitness, banking apps and Scooter Bros (`developer-described`); other app names (`preview-reported`).
11. **Money.** Safe: money gates story progress at points; cash versus bank, cash lost when taken down; fences; garages bought, three vehicle slots at first (`developer-described`). Safehouses not purchasable is a creator's paraphrase of Nelson (`preview-reported`). Other property: `unknown`.
12. **Saving, sleeping, safehouses.** Safehouse interactivity is `developer-described`. Sleep is tracked (`developer-described`, relayed). **Saving is not described anywhere.**
13. **Progression.** Safe: gyms raise muscularity or strength; stats are tracked; overeating and neglect change the body (`developer-described`); the stat list IGN saw (`preview-reported`). No other skill system is evidenced.
14. **Relationship.** Strongly supported by Nelson; optional; states rather than a bar (`developer-described`, relayed); "four-point scale" is Edge's wording (`preview-reported`).
15. **Other.** Focus (Nelson: like Eagle Eye; creator: slows time, weak points); animal study; fishing catch-and-release and hunting for money (relayed Nelson); weather detail (Garbut); police dogs and stinger strips (Edge).
