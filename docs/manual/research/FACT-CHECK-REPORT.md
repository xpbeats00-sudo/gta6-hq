### Chapter-level findings

1. **The primary source is not cited anywhere.** IGN's full Rob Nelson interview (October 7, 2026, read in full via the Yahoo syndication) confirms many claims the manual currently hangs on guides, and corrects two of them (items 2 and 3). It should be added to `sources` and cited first wherever it applies.
2. **"Hot area" is the developer's own term.** The manual says one guide calls it a Hot Area. In the IGN interview Nelson himself, looking at the map, calls the place the police are still hanging around a hot area. VICE says "active search area". Neither is shown to be the on-screen label.
3. **Hollow stars are developer-described, not a disputed guide reading.** Nelson tells IGN that hollow white stars mean the police do not know who you are. The GamesRadar+ image of its "black and white" stars shows outlined (hollow) stars, so the two columns in the star table describe the same thing in row 1.
4. **`PREVIEW-REPORTED` is used loosely.** Section 2.2 defines it as a journalist who personally viewed a build. None of VICE (Koepp relays posts by TGG and TroisCouleurs), GTABase (names no sources) or the GamesRadar+ guide (Wilson cross-reads footage against preview coverage) is first-hand. Where IGN corroborates, re-source to IGN. Where it does not, keep the label only with explicit attribution ("TGG, relayed by VICE"). Claims with no first-hand corroboration I could find: flashing-white and red star meanings, the weapon and camera markers' meanings, whole-map search, police-car trackers, trunk contents, respray, the one-star reduction, Focus through walls.
5. **gta6-wiki.org is a fan wiki** (tier 5 in section 2.1: leads only). Both uses can be replaced: the six stars by the Extended Look itself, the helicopter by Rockstar's own Grassrivers page.
6. **Three numbered-entry texts are word-for-word GTABase sentences** (Face, Weapon, Accomplice). Reword them.
7. **Two "one guide mentions" attributions are wrong**: respray and destroying camera footage each appear in two sources (GTABase and VICE).
8. **Diagrams and the page-4 intro carry no evidence label**, which section 2.2 requires.
9. **The quotation.** "a bit more persistent memory" is verbatim in GamesRadar+ (Catherine Lewis, 2026-09-15), attributed to Rob Nelson, Rockstar North co-studio head, speaking in TGG's video. The quoted span is five words, not nine as CONTENT-STATUS says. It does not appear in the IGN transcript, and I could not watch TGG's video, so the wording rests on GamesRadar+ alone.
10. No GTA V mechanics were found carried over as fact. One series-history claim is doubtful (page 2, "first time in the series").
