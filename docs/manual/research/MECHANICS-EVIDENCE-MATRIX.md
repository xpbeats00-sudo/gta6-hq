# Mechanics Evidence Matrix: police, wanted level, Heat

Agent A claim ledger, 2026-11-11. Source IDs (S1-S12, X1-X8) are defined in `PRIMARY-SOURCE-REGISTER.md`.

## How to read this

- **Evidence type** uses the mission labels. Two cautions specific to this pass:
  - **DEMONSTRATED is not awarded anywhere by Agent A.** The official Extended Look (X1) could not be opened, and I cannot view video frames. Where a writer describes what the footage shows, the row is PREVIEW-REPORTED with the note "footage not verified by Agent A". Someone who can watch X1 should confirm those rows at the timestamp before any manual block says "demonstrated".
  - **DEVELOPER-DESCRIBED (relayed)** means the words are Rob Nelson's but reach us through a visitor reading his own notes (S3), not through a published transcript.
- "Nelson" always means Rob Nelson, Head of Development and Co-Studio Head, Rockstar North. All his statements describe a **July 2026 build** shown hands-off.
- Nelson uses "heat" in more than one way: for the on-screen information the police hold, and for the state of police searching an area ("we have heat now", "we don't have heat now"). The manual should not define Heat more narrowly than he does.
- Quotation is kept to single terms and one short phrase. Everything else is paraphrase.
