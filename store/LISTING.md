## Description

HQ Guide is a fan-made reference for Grand Theft Auto VI. It gathers what has been published
about the game into one place and tells you where each fact comes from.

What is inside:

- An encyclopedia of characters, locations, vehicles, weapons, businesses, activities and more.
  Every entry carries a label saying how well it is supported (official, corroborated, community
  or rumour) and lists its sources.
- A Field Manual: an illustrated guide to the systems shown so far, such as the wanted level,
  health and robberies, with each claim marked by its evidence.
- An interactive map of Leonida with pins for regions, districts and landmarks.
- Intel: the release timeline, editions and prices, and answers to common questions.
- Search across everything.

HQ Guide has no accounts, no advertising and no tracking. It works offline apart from the map,
which downloads its tiles the first time you view each area.

HQ Guide is an unofficial fan project. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by or sponsored by
Rockstar Games or Take-Two Interactive. Grand Theft Auto is a trademark of its owner.
