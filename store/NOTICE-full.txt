GTA 6 HQ is an unofficial fan project.

It is not published, approved or endorsed by Rockstar Games or Take-Two Interactive. Grand Theft Auto and related names, logos, screenshots, artwork and footage belong to their owners and are not licensed to this project.

What the app does on your computer:

- It needs the Microsoft Edge WebView2 Runtime, which Windows 11 already has.
- The map downloads its tiles from gta6-wiki.org the first time each area is viewed, and keeps them in a cache folder in your user profile.
- If Discord is running, it can show "GTA 6 HQ" and the page you are reading on your Discord profile.
- Links to websites and to the community Discord server open in your web browser.
- Updates arrive through the Microsoft Store. The app does not check for updates itself.

There is no telemetry, no advertising and no account. The app collects nothing about you.
