# HQ Guide privacy policy

Last updated: 14 November 2026

HQ Guide is an unofficial fan guide to Grand Theft Auto VI. This policy covers the app as
distributed through the Microsoft Store.

## What the app collects

Nothing. HQ Guide has no accounts, no advertising, no analytics and no telemetry. It does not
collect, store or send any information about you or your device.

## What the app sends over the internet

- **Map tiles.** When you open the map, the app downloads map pictures from `gta6-wiki.org`. That
  server sees an ordinary web request (your IP address and the tile asked for), as any website
  does. The app sends nothing else with it. Tiles are kept on your computer so each one is asked
  for only once.
- **Links you choose to open.** Links to websites and to the community Discord server open in your
  own web browser. What those sites collect is covered by their own policies.

The app makes no other connections. Updates are delivered by the Microsoft Store, not by the app.

## What the app keeps on your computer

- Downloaded map tiles and the app's own display settings (for example, the manual's day or night
  reading mode and the last page you read), in your user profile.

Uninstalling the app removes them.

## Children

The app does not collect information from anyone, including children.

## Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published at the same address with a new date.
